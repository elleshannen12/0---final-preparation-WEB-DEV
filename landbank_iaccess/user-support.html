Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to help you manage your iAccess account.

6 topics
When does my available balance update?

Your dashboard balance is updated after a demo transfer, bill payment, or successful cash-in. Refreshing the page keeps the latest saved balance.

How do I make a fund transfer?

Open Transfer Funds, choose a transfer type, enter the recipient details and amount, then review the information before confirming.

How do I pay a bill?

Choose a biller, provide its account or reference number, enter an amount that meets the displayed minimum, and confirm after reviewing the payment details.

How can I add money to my account?

Choose Cash In on the dashboard, enter an amount, and complete the payment gateway flow. In this prototype, the balance changes only after a successful demo payment.

How do I change the appearance?

Open Settings and use the appearance controls to switch between light and dark mode. Your choice is remembered as you move between pages.

How can I keep my account secure?

Never share your password, PIN, or verification code. For real account concerns, contact LANDBANK through its official support channels.
