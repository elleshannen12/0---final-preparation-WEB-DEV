About Us

Land Bank of the Philippines (LANDBANK) is a government financial institution that provides financial services to the Filipino people, with a special focus on countryside development and supporting the agricultural sector.

Mission

To promote countryside development by providing timely and responsive financial and support services, while remaining a viable and competitive institution.

Vision

By 2028, LANDBANK will be the leading universal bank that promotes inclusive and sustainable development.

Commitment to Secure Digital Banking

Through iAccess, LANDBANK delivers convenient, secure and reliable online banking services. We continuously invest in technology, multi-factor authentication, encryption and customer education to protect your accounts and data.

Security Policy

Encryption Standards

All data transmitted between your device and LANDBANK servers is protected using strong TLS encryption. Sensitive information is stored using industry-accepted cryptographic practices.

Multi-Factor Authentication

iAccess employs multi-factor authentication including passwords, OTPs and security questions to verify your identity before critical actions.

Safe Banking Guidelines

  • Never share your iAccess ID, password or OTP.
  • Always log out after using shared or public devices.
  • Keep your browser and device software up to date.
  • Be cautious of phishing emails or messages claiming to be from LANDBANK.
  • Use Lock My Access, Money Lock and Pause Period features when needed.

Advisory on ATM Use

Security Tips

  • Cover the keypad when entering your PIN.
  • Do not accept help from strangers at the ATM.
  • Check for unusual devices or card skimmers before inserting your card.
  • Memorize your PIN and never write it on or keep it with your card.
  • Report lost or stolen cards immediately to Customer Care.

Best Practices

  • Use ATMs in well-lit, secure locations.
  • Count your cash and secure it before leaving the ATM area.
  • Retain your transaction receipt and check your account regularly.

Warnings

If the ATM retains your card or displays unusual messages, contact LANDBANK Customer Care immediately. Do not leave the machine unattended while your card is inserted.
